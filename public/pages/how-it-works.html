How The Fast Roll Works

A Beltline-native delivery system built for speed, safety, and community.

1. You Buy the Item

Grab ice cream, flowers, snacks, water, candy, or small groceries from any Beltline shop. You can also order online for pickup — just upload the confirmation number.

2. Upload Receipt or Confirmation Number

Riders need proof of purchase to pick up your item. You can upload:

  • A photo of your receipt
  • A screenshot of your order confirmation
  • A pickup code from the store

This ensures riders can walk in, grab your item, and roll out fast.

3. Rider Accepts & Picks Up

A nearby rider accepts your job, grabs your item, and sends a pickup photo for verification.

4. Delivered Fast

Your rider rolls straight to you and sends a dropoff photo. You can tip before or after delivery.

Why This System Works

Riders can bring you cold water or electrolytes fast.

Riders can grab ice cream, candy, or snacks from Beltline shops.

Have flowers delivered right to your spot — smooth and effortless.

Sometimes you just want something brought to you. The Fast Roll makes it easy.

Payments & Payouts

How You Pay

Clients pay through PayPal for delivery fees and tips. It’s secure, fast, and works instantly.

How Riders Get Paid

Riders get paid instantly after each delivery via PayPal. No waiting. No weekly payouts. No delays.
