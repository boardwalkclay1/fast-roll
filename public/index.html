What Is The Fast Roll?

A Beltline-native delivery movement — not a corporate app.

The Fast Roll is built for the Beltline community — not for cars, not for traffic, not for corporate logistics. It’s powered by skaters, longboarders, e-bikes, and OneWheels who love the trail and the people on it.

Built by a real Beltline rider — not a CEO in a tower. Boardwalk_Clay created The Fast Roll to connect the community, support riders, and make the Beltline more interactive and fun.

Every delivery is a chance to meet someone new, share a moment, and build real community connections. Riders aren’t anonymous — they’re part of the Beltline family.

How It Works

Simple, safe, and built for small, low‑risk items.

Grab ice cream, flowers, snacks, water, candy, or small groceries from any Beltline shop — or order online and upload your confirmation number.

Riders need proof of purchase to pick up your item. Upload a receipt photo or order confirmation — whatever the store requires.

A nearby rider accepts your job, grabs your item, and sends a pickup photo for verification.

Your rider rolls straight to you and sends a dropoff photo. You can tip before or after delivery.

Why Use The Fast Roll?

Out on the trail and overheated? Riders can bring you cold water or electrolytes fast.

Craving something sweet? Riders can grab ice cream, candy, or snacks from Beltline shops.

Meeting someone special? Have a rider deliver flowers right to your spot — smooth and effortless.

Sometimes you just want something brought to you. The Fast Roll makes it easy, fast, and fun.

Payments & Payouts

Clients pay through PayPal for delivery fees and tips. It’s secure, fast, and works instantly.

Riders get paid instantly after each delivery via PayPal. No waiting. No weekly payouts. No delays.
